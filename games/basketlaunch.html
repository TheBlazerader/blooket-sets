THE SHOT METER

Green = perfect Timing window

Hold G to build power. Release when the moving marker reaches the green window at the top of the vertical meter. Press C (Player 1) or ; (Player 2) to dunk while carrying the ball in the paint. Timing, range, power, and nearby defenders all affect the make chance. Hold C or ; in the paint to charge the dunk meter, then release. Press Q (Player 1) or L (Player 2) for a crossover. Press V to cycle POWER / WINDMILL / 360 dunk style before dunking. Sprinting drains stamina faster; tired players slow down and recover gradually.
